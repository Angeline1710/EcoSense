What the system is built to do

From a reading to a reasoned next step.

EcoSense connects a near-term estimate to an explanation and a practical what-if, keeping the operational inputs visible throughout.

01 / PREDICT

Estimate emissions

Use energy, grid intensity, production, equipment, shift, and recent readings to predict hourly CO₂ output with an uncertainty range.

02 / EXPLAIN

Understand the drivers

Review SHAP feature contributions to see which submitted operating conditions raised or lowered the prediction.

03 / SIMULATE

Compare a proposed change

Adjust selected parameters in a what-if scenario and compare the estimated emissions with the submitted baseline.

Made for the people closest to the process.

Operations managers can evaluate shift-level conditions. Plant engineers can investigate emission drivers. Sustainability and ESG teams can use the estimates as an analytical aid alongside their validated reporting process.

Prototype scope: The bundled telemetry is synthetic demonstration data. The prediction uses values submitted by the user; this MVP does not connect to live factory sensors, issue alerts, or produce an audited emissions report.
